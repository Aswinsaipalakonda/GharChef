# GharChef Design System

Version: 1.0

---

# Design Philosophy

GharChef should feel like a premium startup product instead of a college project.

The overall experience should combine the simplicity of Swiggy, the premium feel of Airbnb, the clean spacing of Apple, and the modern cards of Uber Eats.

Every page should feel light, warm, welcoming and food-focused.

The interface should emphasize food photography with soft shadows, rounded corners, generous whitespace, and subtle animations.

Never create bulky dashboards or oversized typography.

The UI should always feel elegant.
